### What is Night's Black Agents?
*Night’s Black Agents* is a (vampire) spy thriller game, in which highly trained and  competent intelligence operatives uncover a conspiracy larger — and stranger — than they were ever meant to see. The player characters are not amateurs or investigators stumbling into danger, but experienced professionals with the skills, contacts, and instincts to operate in hostile environments. 

At its core, the game is about **investigation, paranoia, and survival**. Players follow leads, piece together fragments of information, and gradually map out a hidden network of power. The structure encourages proactive play: throughout your investigation, you must decide decide where to go, what risks to take, and which threads to pull. 

Mechanically, *Night’s Black Agents* uses the GUMSHOE system, which is designed to support investigative play. If you have the right skill and look in the right place, you will find the core clues needed to move forward. The challenge lies not in *finding* information, but figuring out what it *means*. In deciding how far to push, how many resources to spend, and what to do with what you uncover.
