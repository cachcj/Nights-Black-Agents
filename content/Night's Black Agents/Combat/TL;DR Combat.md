This section provides a high-level overview of the combat mechanics, which should be more than enough for players to play the game. 

Throughout the game, the Director will let you know of any modifiers or changes to these rules, or other additional options that have become available to you. If you wish to perform any fancier manoeuvres, such as shooting a gun out of your opponent's hand or smashing your opponent into a bookshelf with a judo throw, feel free to bring this up to the Director.

### Order of Battle
A fight progresses in rounds, detailed below:

1. The initiatives of each combatant is determined, with Surprised combatants going last.
2. On their turn, a combatant rolls 1d6 against their target's Hit Threshold, spending points from their relevant General Ability pool (Hand-to-Hand, Weapons, Shooting) to increase their roll result.
3. On a successful hit, the combatant rolls 1d6, and modifies this result with the damage modifier of their weapon. The target subtracts this from their Health pool.
4. This continues until each combatant has had a turn, and a new round begins from Step 2.

<br/>  

### Injury
When your Health pool drops below 0, you may be prompted to make Consciousness rolls. Roll 1d6 against a Difficulty equal to the absolute value of your current Health pool.

> When your Health pool is:
> 
> - *Between 0 and -5:* You are **Hurt**, and the Difficulty of all tests and contests, including opponents' Hit Thresholds, are increased by 1.
> - *Between -6 and -11:* You are **Seriously Wounded**, and are no longer able to fight.
> - *-12 or below:* You are **dead**.

> [!tip] First Aid  
> A character with the Medic ability can improve your condition by spending Medic pool points. For each point spent, you regain 2 Health points (1 Health point if administering First Aid to yourself).
