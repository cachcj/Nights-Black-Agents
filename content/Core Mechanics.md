Night's Black Agents separates the business of discovering information with that of confronting the opposition. Your Investigative Abilities are your bread and butter when attempting the former, while the large majority of all other actions will depend on your General Abilities.

### Clue, Clues, Clues
Figuring out the conspiracy, or planning an operation, is hard enough without someone withholding half the pieces from you. 

GUMSHOE therefore makes the finding of clues all but automatic, as long as you get to the right place in the story and have the right ability. The fun part is when the players try to put the components of the puzzle together. If you get stuck, go out and have a sniff around!

> [!tip] ***Gathering Clues...***
> Is simple! All you got to do is:   
> - Get yourself into a scene where relevant information can be gathered
> - Have the right ability to discover the clue, and
> - Tell the Director you're using it.
>  
> As long as you do these things, you will never fail to gain a piece of necessary information.  

When gathering clues, you can specify exactly what you intend to achieve...
> *"I use Chemistry to test the blood sample for silver."*


  <br/>
... or convey a wider speculation...  

> *"I use Human Terrain to find a respected priest in the village who's worried about the disappearance.*

<br/>
... or even engage in more general informational fishing.  

> *"I use Notice to search the alley."* or *"I use Art History to check out the portrait."*


#### Spends and Benefits
Certain clues allow you to gain special benefits by spending points from the relevant Investigative Ability pool.
