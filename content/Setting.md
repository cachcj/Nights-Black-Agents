## 🧭 The Geopolitical Situation

Berlin is where the Cold War is most visible.

The world is split between East and West — led by the **Soviet Union** and the **United States** — and Berlin sits directly on that fault line.

Something is changing.

Reforms under **Mikhail Gorbachev** have loosened control in Eastern Europe. Borders are shifting. Protests are growing. Governments are hesitating.

No one agrees on what happens next.

- The system might reform
- It might collapse
- Or it might lash out

The fall of the **Berlin Wall** is _not_ a certainty — not yet. From the ground, things feel unstable, not inevitable.

### What you hear

- “Something’s breaking in the East.”
- “Moscow isn’t in control anymore.”
- “This could turn violent.”

### What you feel

- Tension without a clear source
- Events moving faster than intelligence can track
- Everyone trying to stay ahead of something they don’t fully understand

## 🏙️ The City

Berlin is two worlds forced together.

---

### West Berlin

A Western enclave inside East Germany.

Around **Berlin Zoo** (Zoologischer Garten):

- Crowded streets, transport hubs, nightlife
- Easy to disappear into crowds
- Good for meetings, surveillance, and handoffs

Elsewhere:

- **Kurfürstendamm** → shops, cafés, Western excess
- **Tiergarten** → quiet spaces, useful for dead drops

**Feel:** loud, fast, open — but artificial. You are surrounded.

---

### East Berlin

Controlled, watchful, and quieter.

Key areas:

- **Alexanderplatz** → public gatherings, transport hub
- Government districts and wide, exposed boulevards

What stands out:

- Fewer goods, longer queues
- Less noise, fewer lights
- People who measure their words

**Feel:** ordered, tense, observed.

---

### The Wall

The **Berlin Wall** is always there.

- Guard towers, floodlights, patrols
- Crossing points like **Checkpoint Charlie**

Crossing is possible — but:

- You are documented
- You are watched
- You are remembered

---

### Operating Reality

- The **British Embassy East Berlin** gives you _official cover_
- West Berlin gives you _operational freedom_

If things go wrong, those two don’t always align.
